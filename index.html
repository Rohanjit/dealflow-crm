Two pipelines in one place: outreach to win the mandate, then execution all the way to closing.

Six modules. Everything a deal team touches.

  • Investors

    Funds, family offices and strategics with their sectors, ticket sizes, geography and who owns the relationship.

  • Contacts

    People on the investor side and the company side, in one list, linked to their firm.

  • Outreach

    A drag-and-drop board for winning mandates. Each company once, every approach logged.

  • Active Deals

    Deal team, fees and a per-deal investor tracker with funnel counts and suggested investors.

  • Tasks

    Follow-ups assigned to people, linked to any record, with in-app reminders.

  • Vault

    NDAs, teasers and term sheets stored on your own server, behind a separate PIN.

Your data, on your machine.

Dealflow isn't a hosted service. Install it with Docker on a laptop for yourself, or on your firm's server for the whole team.

Data is encrypted at rest, uploads are virus-scanned, and backups run every day.

  • MIT licensed
  • Role-based access
  • Excel import and export
  • Multi-currency
  • Daily backups
